PayFreight.io

Next-Generation Automated Logistics Settlement Ecosystem
Executive Summary: PayFreight.io simplifies and accelerates global freight settlement by integrating seamless automated payments with CargoNet's trade network.

1. The Problem

  • Payment Delays: Freight settlement processes routinely take 30 to 90 days, locking up critical working capital for carriers.
  • High Transaction Friction: Cross-border payments involve expensive wire fees, manual invoicing, and currency conversion losses.
  • Lack of Integration: Freight booking platforms and financial settlement systems operate in silos.

2. The Solution

  • PayFreight.io: Instant or near-instant freight payment settlement platform utilizing smart workflows and Web3/crypto rails where applicable.
  • Integrated Ecosystem: Connected directly with CargoNet to automatically trigger payment clearing upon verified freight delivery milestones.

3. Market Opportunity

  • Global Freight & Logistics Market: Over $8 Trillion annually.
  • Target Audience: Freight forwarders, independent carriers, brokers, and cross-border logistics operators seeking instant liquidity and lower fees.
