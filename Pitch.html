The Multi-Billion Dollar Logistics Bottleneck

  • 30–90 Day Payment Terms: Carriers and freight forwarders suffer from severe cash flow droughts while hauling cargo.
  • High Friction & Fees: Cross-border payments between USA and Europe face slow wire delays and high FX conversion costs.
  • Fragmented Systems: Freight exchanges match loads instantly, but financial clearing remains manual, slow, and isolated.

The PayFreight Solution & Ecosystem

An embedded smart contract settlement engine bridging traditional transport data with instant digital clearing.

Founding Anchor & Architect:

Built by CargoTrans.net, natively integrating established exchange platforms TransNet and CargoNet to automate milestone payouts.

Sustainable Business Model

  • Volume Tier Subscriptions: Prepaid monthly/annual packages tailored for high-volume freight brokers and exchanges.
  • Dynamic Settlement Fees: Standard 1.5% transaction fee per cleared freight invoice, discounted down to 0.5% for high-volume enterprise partners.
  • Hybrid Web 2.5 Rails: Supporting traditional SEPA/ACH bank payouts alongside crypto stablecoin settlements.
